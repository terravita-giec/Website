Method

How the maps and figures were produced, and what each layer shows.

Land-cover classification
Each year's map assigns every pixel to one of nine classes, including trees, crops, shrubland, and built-up land.
New built-up land
Pixels that changed to built-up between two years. Persistent built-up land was already built-up at the start and remained so.
Built-up likelihood
The estimated probability that each pixel is built-up in a given year.
Development pressure and potential loss
Screening layers that highlight where growth is most likely to continue, and where built-up land is most likely to be lost.
