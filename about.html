WHO WE ARE

Geospatial intelligence for a changing world.

TerraVita Environmental & Geospatial Consultancy works at the intersection of geospatial science, environmental analysis and technology.

We use Geographic Information Systems (GIS), satellite remote sensing, spatial modelling and data analytics to understand landscapes, monitor environmental changes and support informed planning.

Our approach combines scientific thinking, geospatial technologies and practical analysis to develop clear, evidence-based outputs for research, planning and project-level decision support.

Based in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, TerraVita aims to support projects across India and international markets.

LEADERSHIP

Janvi Patel

Founder | TerraVita Environmental & Geospatial Consultancy

TerraVita is founded by Janvi Patel, an Applied Geology postgraduate with an academic and professional background in geospatial analysis, GIS, remote sensing and environmental research.

Her interests include satellite-based environmental monitoring, land use and land cover analysis, urban environmental assessment, geological applications and the integration of Python and GeoAI into geospatial workflows.

Through TerraVita, she aims to connect scientific understanding with practical geospatial applications and contribute to evidence-based environmental and spatial decision-making.
