WHO WE ARE

Geospatial intelligence for a changing world.

TerraVita Environmental & Geospatial Consultancy works at the intersection of Earth observation, geospatial technology, environmental research and data analytics.

We use GIS, satellite remote sensing, spatial modelling and Python-based workflows to study landscapes, identify patterns and support informed planning.

Our work focuses on making complex spatial information easier to understand through maps, analytical outputs, technical reports and decision-support insights.

Based in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India, TerraVita aims to support projects across India and international markets.

OUR EXPERTISE

Connecting Earth data with real-world applications.

Our consultancy focuses on six interconnected areas of geospatial and environmental work.

01 / GIS

GIS & Spatial Analysis

Spatial mapping, geodatabase development, digitization, spatial modelling and location-based analysis.

02 / EO

Remote Sensing

Satellite image processing, land-use and land-cover mapping, vegetation indices and change detection.

03 / ENV

Environmental Analytics

Watershed, wetlands, groundwater, flood exposure, land degradation and environmental assessment support.

04 / URBAN

Urban Intelligence

Urban growth, land suitability, green cover, urban heat analysis and infrastructure-related mapping.

05 / GEO

Geological Intelligence

Geological spatial data analysis, terrain assessment, mineral prospectivity and geological mapping support.

06 / AI

GeoAI & Automation

Python-based geospatial workflows, spatial data analytics, machine learning and image analysis applications.
