REMOTE SENSING · GIS · DUE DILIGENCE

Satellite-Based Land & Environmental Due Diligence

A preliminary screening of a hypothetical 25–50 hectare development site in Ahmedabad’s Shela–Ghuma–Sanathal–Telav peri-urban corridor, combining land-cover change, vegetation, water, terrain, access and constraint analysis into one suitability view.

Ahmedabad, Gujarat Analysis years: 2015 · 2020 · 2025 Sentinel-2 + Landsat 10 final maps

THE QUESTION

Is this land relatively suitable for development, and what environmental constraints should be understood first?

This is a demonstration of TerraVita’s analytical workflow on a hypothetical site. It is a screening study, not a certified flood-risk, legal or environmental clearance assessment. Final methods and datasets are selected according to each client’s study area and objectives.

METHODOLOGY

From data to decision

Each stage produces a map or table, and every statistic is calculated directly from the GIS data.

01

Prepare satellite data

Check cloud cover, apply cloud masking, clip to the AOI and buffer, reproject to one CRS, and align imagery across years using comparable seasons.

Output: analysis-ready rasters and a preprocessing log

02

Land use / land cover

Supervised classification (such as Random Forest) into built-up, agriculture, vegetation, barren or open land, water and other relevant classes.

Output: LULC map, area and percentage by class, confusion matrix, accuracy

03

Historical change, 2015 → 2020 → 2025

Compare classified maps to identify transitions such as agriculture to built-up, open land to built-up and vegetation to built-up.

Output: change map, change statistics, transition matrix

04

NDVI vegetation analysis

Calculate NDVI for each year and report minimum, maximum and mean values, area by NDVI class, and vegetation loss and gain.

Output: NDVI maps, statistics, interpretation

05

NDWI / MNDWI and water proximity

Detect water bodies and potential wet areas, then run proximity analysis at 50, 100, 250 and 500 m. These are screening distances, not legal setbacks. Satellite-detected water is kept separate from officially designated wetlands.

Output: water map, proximity map, statistics

06

Built-up expansion

Use LULC, NDBI where useful, and historical imagery to find existing built-up areas, new development, direction of growth and development around the site.

Output: expansion map, growth statistics

07

Terrain and flood / drainage screening

Derive elevation, slope, flow direction, flow accumulation and distance from water from the DEM. Where data allows, add rainfall, flood history and land use for a preliminary susceptibility map.

Output: DEM, slope, flow maps, flood screening map, weighting method

08

Road accessibility

Classify highways, primary, secondary and local roads, and measure approximate distance from the site to the nearest highway, main road, settlement and key infrastructure.

Output: accessibility map, distance table

09

Environmental constraints

Combine water, wet areas, high vegetation, flood susceptibility, steep slopes, built-up areas and protected areas from authoritative sources.

Output: constraint layers and a combined constraints map

10

Site suitability and risk

A documented weighted-overlay method: input layers, standardisation, weights, scoring, final calculation and classification into relatively more suitable, moderately constrained and relatively more constrained areas.

Output: final suitability map and calculation tables

The flood analysis is a preliminary screening and not a certified flood-risk assessment. Weights and scoring will be published alongside the final map.
