SITE INTELLIGENCE

Satellite-Based Land Due Diligence

A structured site-screening framework combining land use, historical change and environmental constraint layers.

THE QUESTION

What spatial and environmental factors should be reviewed before a land or development decision?

This workflow is designed as an early-stage screening tool. Regulatory, legal, engineering and field verification should be incorporated where required.

WORKFLOW

01Define site boundary and buffer
02Map current land use / land cover
03Assess historical change
04Overlay water, flood and environmental constraints
05Synthesize risk and suitability layers

KEY OUTPUTS

↗LULC map
↗5–10 year change analysis
↗Water / wetland proximity
↗Flood-risk overlay
↗Site constraint map
↗2–3 page decision brief
