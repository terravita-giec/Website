§ APPROACH

How a project moves

  1. 01

    Survey & data collection

    Field surveys, drone and satellite capture, sensor deployment. We collect what the site actually needs, not a generic dataset.

  2. 02

    Spatial analysis

    GIS modelling, habitat and terrain analysis, and change detection turn raw data into a legible picture of the land.

  3. 03

    Assessment & reporting

    Findings become documentation your regulators, board, or funders can read and rely on — no translation required.

  4. 04

    Implementation support

    Monitoring plans and adaptive management so the work stays accurate long after the report is filed.
