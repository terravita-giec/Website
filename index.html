ABOUT TERRAVITA

Spatial intelligence for a changing planet.

TerraVita Environmental & Geospatial Consultancy combines Earth observation, remote sensing, GIS, environmental analytics, geological interpretation and GeoAI to support data-driven decision-making.

Our workflows are designed around real project questions: Where is change happening? What are the environmental constraints? How is a site evolving? Where are risks concentrated? What should the next decision consider?

From satellite-based monitoring to spatial modelling and analytical reporting, our focus is on converting geospatial information into meaningful project insights.

OUR SERVICES

Geospatial solutions for real-world challenges.

Integrated satellite, GIS and analytical workflows designed to support environmental, urban, infrastructure and geological projects.

GIS Mapping & Spatial Analysis

Spatial databases, thematic mapping, digitisation, spatial overlays, site analysis and suitability modelling.

Remote Sensing & Earth Observation

Satellite image processing, NDVI, NDWI, LULC, change detection and environmental monitoring.

Environmental & Land Assessment

Land-use assessment, water and wetland analysis, flood exposure, terrain and environmental risk mapping.

Urban & Infrastructure Analytics

Urban growth, land surface temperature, heat-risk assessment, green-space analysis and infrastructure planning support.

Geological & Resource Analytics

Terrain interpretation, geological mapping, remote-sensing-based exploration indicators and prospectivity modelling.

GeoAI & Geospatial Automation

Python-based workflows, machine learning, spatial modelling and automated geospatial data processing.
