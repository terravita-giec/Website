Why geospatial intelligence matters

From raw signal to decision intelligence

Satellite data on its own is not an answer. TerraVita moves it through four stages so what reaches your desk is something you can act on, defend and revisit.

  1. 1

    Raw Satellite Data

    Optical, radar, thermal and elevation feeds acquired, ortho-rectified and calibrated to a common datum.

    → Analysis-ready imagery

  2. 2

    GIS Analysis

    Vectors, rasters and terrain joined into one queryable spatial model with your own records folded in.

    → Structured spatial model

  3. 3

    AI & Analytics

    Machine learning and statistics surface patterns, anomalies and multi-temporal change at scale.

    → Measured change and risk

  4. 4

    Decision Intelligence

    Ranked, mapped and documented recommendations your board, regulator or lender can act on.

    → Defensible decision
